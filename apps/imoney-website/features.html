One coin. Three jobs. Nothing bolted on.

Most coins do one thing well and leave the rest to outside apps. Internet Money builds the whole toolkit into the coin itself: paying, saving safely, making tokens and trading them. Every computer on the network checks the same short rulebook, so there is no app in between to trust, and no company holding your money.

§2Spend it

Everything a shop needs, with nobody taking a cut.

  • Fast and cheap

    A new block every five seconds. A payment costs a fraction of a cent, whatever its size.

  • Order numbers on every payment

    Each payment can carry the order it pays for, signed by the customer, so a shop always knows which order was paid.

  • Check payments yourself

    A full node runs on a cheap server. A shop sees its money arrive with its own eyes, instead of trusting a payment company.

  • Refunds, not chargebacks

    Payments can't be pulled back by a third party. Giving money back is the shop's choice, and the refund is linked to the order.

  • Escrow with a referee built in

    The buyer pays into escrow and releases it when the goods arrive. In a dispute, a referee decides, and can only ever pay the buyer or the seller.

  • Subscriptions built in

    A customer allows a shop to collect a set amount once a period, and can cancel at any time. The shop can never take more.

  • Run a node, earn a share built in

    A wallet or shop that runs its own node can be named on a payment and receives half its fee. Running the network pays.

  • Plugs into your shop

    A WooCommerce plugin for WordPress, and a few lines of JavaScript for any other site.

§3Save it

Built to hold value, and to keep it yours.

  • Savings vaults built in

    Money in a vault can only leave after a waiting period you choose. If someone steals your phone and starts a withdrawal, your backup key cancels it.

  • Shared wallets

    A wallet that needs two of three people (or any number you choose) to approve each payment. Good for families, partners and companies.

  • No coins made in advance

    Every coin is mined. No premine, no sale to insiders, no share for the developers.

  • A schedule nobody can change

    The reward halves every four years, four times, then stays small forever. After year sixteen, new coins add less than 1% a year, and that share keeps falling.

  • Security that doesn't run out

    Because a small reward continues, miners are always paid to protect the network, even when fees are low. A fixed cap leaves that to fees alone.

  • Final after twelve hours

    A payment older than twelve hours can never be undone, however much computing power someone brings.

  • Mined on ordinary graphics cards

    Mining reads randomly across gigabytes of memory, which keeps it open to anyone with a gaming card rather than a few specialist chip makers.

  • Open and checkable

    All the code is open source, and anyone can run a node and check every coin from the first block.

§4Build on it

Tokens and trading, with nothing to write or audit.

  • Your own token built in

    Loyalty points, a game currency, a community coin. Give it a name and an amount, and it exists. You decide whether more can ever be made.

  • A built-in market built in

    Put coins or tokens up at your price, and anyone can buy some or all of them. You get paid, or nothing happens: no exchange holds your money.

  • Swaps with Ethereum

    Trade IMN for coins on Ethereum and similar chains directly with another person. Either both sides happen or neither does.

  • No smart contracts to break

    Tokens, trading, escrow and the rest are fixed rules every node checks, not programs anyone can deploy. There is far less code for anything to go wrong in.

  • No bridges, no wrapped coins

    Tokens live on Internet Money itself, and swaps happen chain to chain. Nothing is locked up with a middleman to make it work.

  • Tools for developers

    A JavaScript SDK, a browser wallet library, a cookbook of examples, and a node with a plain web API.

§5How we compare

Others do one or two of these well. We put all three in the coin.

Bitcoin is the strongest store of value. Ethereum can do almost anything, through programs anyone can write, and the risks that come with them. Kaspa is very fast. Internet Money's bet is that most people need a short list of things done well, built in and checked by everyone.

§6The honest part

Where we're strong, and where we're not yet.

Why choose us

  • The everyday tools of money in one coin, checked by every node
  • Far less code to go wrong than an open smart-contract platform
  • A fair start: every coin mined, none set aside
  • Mining open to anyone with a graphics card
  • People who run nodes for others get paid for it

What to weigh

  • It's a test network: test coins have no value, and there's nothing to buy yet
  • Young and small next to networks that have run for years
  • An outside test of its security has started; a full independent audit hasn't
  • Built-in features mean a fixed toolkit, not anything you can program
  • Some features reach the public test network with its next update
