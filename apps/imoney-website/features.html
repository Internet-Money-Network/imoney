§2Only here

Three things the big networks don't do.

Savings a thief can't empty.

Every withdrawal from a vault waits a day, or however long you choose. If it isn't you, your backup key cancels it.

Run a node, get paid.

A wallet or shop that runs its own node earns half the fee of every payment it handles.

Built in, so there's less to break.

Elsewhere, tokens, trading and escrow are separate apps, each one more thing to trust. Here they're rules every node checks.
