Engineering Architecture

Designed around verifiable commodity money principles without ICOs or founder allocations.

5-Second Parallel BlockDAG

Unlike linear chains that orphan simultaneous blocks, GHOSTDAG orders parallel blocks into a single deterministic ledger. Honest blocks become blue, enabling 5-second merchant settlements without chain bloat.

Memory-Hard ASIC Resistance

The "Money Printer" algorithm requires high-bandwidth pseudo-random lookups across large memory datasets. ASICs cannot gain significant efficiency advantages over consumer GPUs without buying retail GDDR6/HBM memory.

Lightweight Pure-Rust Node

Built on embedded redb ACID storage with SegWit-style witness separation. Pruned full nodes require approximately 5–8 GB of disk space indefinitely.

Drop-In Merchant Payments

Zero intermediary fees. Funds settle directly into the merchant's non-custodial address.

The official @imoney/sdk runs in both Node.js and browser runtimes. It includes a WebSocket listener that triggers as soon as payment is detected in the BlockDAG mempool or block.
