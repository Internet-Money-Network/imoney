How it works

Your main address needs two signatures to spend from: Ed25519, the kind used everywhere today, and SQIsign, a new kind built to resist quantum computers. Both keys come from this wallet's recovery phrase. SQIsign is new and not yet audited; because both signatures are needed, your money stays protected even if one kind is ever broken.
